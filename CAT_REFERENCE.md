### Recognizable Features

- Young British Shorthair with a compact, rounded body and dense short coat.
- Broad rounded forehead and full cheeks, but not an exaggerated flat face.
- Large round grey-green eyes with very dark rims and wide spacing.
- Small pink triangular nose, short muzzle, white chin, and rounded white whisker pads.
- Medium triangular ears, set fairly wide and upright; pale pink inner ears.
- Warm golden-orange shaded cap, back, flanks, outer forelegs, and upper tail.
- Clear white blaze around the muzzle that expands into a broad white throat,
  chest, belly, and inner legs.
- Golden patches centered on the tops of the front paws, with white toe edges.
- Thick plush tail, close to body length, tapering softly to a dark brown tip.
- Short-to-medium sturdy legs and rounded paws; chest is deep and body is compact.

### Proportion Guide

- Head width: approximately 0.82 of shoulder width in the frontal photo.
- Eye diameter: approximately 0.19 of visible head width.
- Eye spacing: approximately one eye width between the inner eye edges.
- Ear height: approximately 0.31 of head height; ears remain visibly prominent.
- Muzzle projects only slightly beyond the eye plane.
- Tail base is thick; the final 12-15% is distinctly darker.

### Modeling Priorities

1. Large, widely spaced dark-rimmed eyes and the short pink-nosed muzzle.
2. Golden cap/back paired with a broad white chest and white lower face.
3. Compact body, sturdy legs, rounded paws, and thick dark-tipped tail.
4. Dense short-fur impression through silhouette, roughness, and fine bump.

### Known Limits

- The current references do not show a clean rear view or full underside.
- Hidden markings should remain simple and symmetric until a new photo shows them.
